G1 Prep Ontario

Privacy policy

Effective date: 25 August 2026

G1 Prep Ontario (ca.g1prep.ontario) is a free Ontario G1 practice app. It is a study aid. It is not affiliated with the Ministry of Transportation or DriveTest.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no cloud sync.

This version does not include ads, analytics, or crash reporting.

Data on your device

Practice progress stays on your phone:

  • last and best scores for each test
  • in-progress tests so you can resume
  • missed question IDs for Review mistakes
  • per-question seen and missed counts used to order review

This data is stored locally on the device and is not sent to us or to any third party.

Reset and uninstall

Use About → Reset progress in the app to delete local scores and missed questions. Uninstalling the app also removes this data.

Children

The app is rated Everyone and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Contact

For privacy questions about this app, use the contact email on the Google Play listing for G1 Prep Ontario.

Changes

If we add ads, accounts, analytics, or any network collection, we will update this policy and the Play Console Data safety form before that version ships.
