G1 Prep Ontario

Privacy policy

Effective date: 28 August 2026

G1 Prep Ontario (ca.g1prep.ontario) is a free Ontario G1 practice app. It is a study aid. It is not affiliated with the Ministry of Transportation or DriveTest.

Data we collect

There is no account, no sign-in, and no cloud sync of your practice progress.

The app shows banner ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and Google may collect device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, and coarse app or device activity needed to serve and measure ads. That data is handled under Google’s advertising policies, not by us as a separate backend.

We do not run our own analytics or crash reporting.

Data on your device

Practice progress stays on your phone:

  • last and best scores for each test
  • in-progress tests so you can resume
  • missed question IDs for Review mistakes
  • per-question seen and missed counts used to order review

This progress data is stored locally on the device and is not sent to us. Ad requests go to Google’s ad network over the internet.

Reset and uninstall

Use About → Reset progress in the app to delete local scores and missed questions. Uninstalling the app also removes this data. Resetting progress does not control AdMob or advertising identifiers on the device; use your device’s ad or privacy settings for that.

Children

The app is rated Everyone. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are served by Google AdMob subject to Google’s policies for the Play Store rating.

Contact

For privacy questions about this app, use the contact email on the Google Play listing for G1 Prep Ontario.

Changes

If we add accounts, analytics, or other network collection beyond AdMob banners, we will update this policy and the Play Console Data safety form before that version ships.
